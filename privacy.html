Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

1. Introduction

SocialXchange ("we", "our", "the app") is a campus exchange application for university students. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

When you use SocialXchange, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your name, university email address, and user ID, obtained through Microsoft (MSAL) university authentication.
  • Profile Information: Profile photo (if uploaded).
  • User-Generated Content: Listings, lost & found reports, order posts, requests, and messages you create within the app.
  • Photos: Images you upload for marketplace listings or lost & found reports.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your identity as a university student
  • Display your listings, reports, and messages to other users
  • Enable communication between buyers, sellers, and other users
  • Send notifications about your activity (messages, orders, listings)

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase cloud infrastructure with row-level security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only access their own private data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS using industry-standard TLS encryption.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only visible to other authenticated university users within the app as necessary for the app's functionality (e.g., your listing details are visible to other users).

6. Third-Party Services

SocialXchange uses the following third-party services:

  • Microsoft Authentication (MSAL): For secure university account sign-in.
  • Supabase: For secure data storage and real-time features.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

SocialXchange is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 17.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
