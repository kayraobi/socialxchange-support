Support & Help

About

SocialXchange is a campus exchange app for university students. Buy and sell items, report lost and found belongings, coordinate orders from abroad, and message fellow students — all in one place.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign in?
Use your university Microsoft account to sign in securely.


How do I list an item for sale?
Go to the Marketplace tab, tap the + button, fill in the details, add photos, and post your listing.


How do I report a lost or found item?
Go to the Lost & Found tab, tap Report, choose whether the item is lost or found, and fill in the details.


Is SocialXchange free?
Yes, SocialXchange is completely free to use.
